# Build Content Growth Engine

## Workflow

1. Define the audience, job, journey stage, desired behavior, offer, channels, and success metric.
2. Build content themes from evidenced customer questions, objections, triggers, and search or community demand.
3. Map themes to formats, funnel roles, distribution paths, and conversion mechanisms.
4. Create a sustainable production and approval cadence with clear owners.
5. Write channel-native copy using the message hierarchy and evidence available.
6. Repurpose strong ideas without copying the same format unchanged across channels.
7. Instrument reach, engagement, qualified action, conversion, retention, and assisted impact as relevant.
8. Review results, capture learnings, and update themes, formats, hooks, offers, and distribution.

Read [references/copy-and-content-system.md](references/copy-and-content-system.md) when producing copy or an editorial operating system.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent customer quotes, proof points, statistics, or product capabilities.
- Separate known audience insight from creative hypotheses.
- Match copy to channel, awareness level, and intended action.
- Prioritize useful, distinctive content over publishing volume.
- Treat content as a measurable system, not a calendar alone.

## Required Output

1. Audience and journey context
2. Content thesis and strategic themes
3. Message hierarchy and proof inventory
4. Format and distribution system
5. Editorial and production workflow
6. Content or copy deliverables requested
7. Repurposing map
8. Conversion paths and calls to action
9. Measurement and experiment plan
10. Learning backlog
