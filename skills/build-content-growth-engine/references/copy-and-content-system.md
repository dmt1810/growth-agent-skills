# Copy and Content System

## Message hierarchy

Define audience, painful or desired situation, promised outcome, differentiating mechanism, proof, objection handling, offer, and next action. Mark missing proof explicitly.

## Content brief

Capture objective, audience, journey stage, single takeaway, angle, supporting evidence, structure, format, channel, call to action, owner, deadline, and success metric.

## Copy review

Check clarity, specificity, relevance, credibility, voice consistency, information order, friction, channel fit, and strength of the next action. Remove unsupported superlatives and vague claims.

## Experiment register

Track asset, variable tested, hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, audience, dates, result, confidence, and next action. Avoid attributing impact to content when tracking cannot support the claim.
