# Search Audit Schema

## Issue register

| Finding | Evidence | Surface affected | Business impact | Confidence | Effort | Dependency | Recommendation | Validation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Review areas

- Access: robots controls, sitemaps, status codes, rendering, canonicals, redirects, pagination, indexation
- Experience: mobile usability, performance, accessibility, intrusive friction
- Architecture: hierarchy, navigation, URL patterns, internal links, orphaned content
- Relevance: intent fit, entity coverage, titles, headings, answer clarity, freshness, differentiation
- Trust: authorship, citations, original evidence, policies, reputation, external references
- Conversion: journey continuity, offer relevance, calls to action, measurement

## Prioritization

Score impact, confidence, effort, dependency, and time to learn. Keep observed facts separate from recommendations and expected outcomes.
