# Executive Reporting Standard

## Answer-first narrative

Lead with:

1. What happened?
2. Why did it happen, with confidence labels?
3. Why does it matter?
4. What should be decided or done next?

## Recommendation standard

For each recommendation include evidence, expected mechanism, owner, timing, success metric, guardrail, and next decision point. Do not express an expected uplift without a defensible basis and uncertainty range.

## Data-quality note

Report coverage, freshness, material exclusions, definition changes, attribution limitations, and reconciliation status. If a flaw could reverse a conclusion, flag the conclusion as unsafe for decision-making.
