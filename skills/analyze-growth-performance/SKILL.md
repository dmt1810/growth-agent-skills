# Analyze Growth Performance

## Workflow

1. Clarify the decision, audience, reporting period, comparison, targets, and definitions.
2. Audit source completeness, grain, freshness, joins, exclusions, attribution rules, and metric consistency before analysis.
3. Reconcile totals and definitions. Do not proceed silently through material data-quality failures.
4. Calculate outcomes, leading indicators, funnel rates, unit economics, cohorts, segments, and channel contributions as relevant.
5. Compare against targets, prior periods, seasonality, forecasts, or suitable benchmarks.
6. Diagnose drivers using decomposition and segment analysis. Separate correlation from causation.
7. State what changed, why it likely changed, why it matters, and what decision follows.
8. Produce an answer-first executive report with evidence, caveats, owners, and next checks.

Read [references/executive-reporting.md](references/executive-reporting.md) before presenting the final report.

## Guardrails

- Never fabricate missing rows, targets, benchmarks, or attribution.
- Preserve numerator, denominator, population, window, and currency in metric definitions.
- Label observed drivers, plausible drivers, and causal claims separately.
- Show absolute values with percentage changes when decision-relevant.
- Recommend further instrumentation or analysis when the available data cannot answer the question.

## Required Output

1. Executive answer and decision implications
2. Metric status versus target and comparison period
3. Validated drivers and segment detail
4. Funnel, channel, campaign, or cohort findings as relevant
5. Risks, guardrails, and data-quality limitations
6. Recommended actions with owners and expected measurement
7. Open questions and required follow-up analysis
8. Metric definitions and source notes
