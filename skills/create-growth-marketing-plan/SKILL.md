# Create Growth Marketing Plan

## Workflow

1. Clarify the business outcome, baseline, target, horizon, audience, offer, constraints, and decision owners.
2. Audit the quality of the inputs. Label facts, assumptions, and missing information.
3. Translate the target into a measurable growth model across acquisition, activation, retention, revenue, and referral as applicable.
4. Choose the highest-leverage audiences, problems, positioning, journeys, and channels.
5. Build strategic pillars and a sequenced portfolio of campaigns, programs, and experiments.
6. Allocate resources by expected impact, confidence, effort, learning value, and strategic fit.
7. Define KPIs, leading indicators, guardrails, measurement cadence, owners, and decision rules.
8. State dependencies, risks, assumptions, and research required before major commitments.

Read [references/plan-template.md](references/plan-template.md) when producing the final plan.

## Guardrails

- Do not create channel tactics before establishing the audience, problem, offer, and objective.
- Do not imply precision where baselines or conversion data are missing.
- Separate committed programs from experiments and exploratory bets.
- Make tradeoffs explicit; a plan is a set of choices, not a list of every possible activity.
- Connect every initiative to a KPI and decision owner.

## Required Output

1. Executive strategy and choices
2. Starting point and assumptions
3. Growth model and targets
4. Priority audiences, problems, and positioning
5. Strategic pillars
6. Funnel and channel roles
7. Initiative and experiment roadmap
8. Budget or resource allocation scenarios
9. KPI tree, guardrails, and reporting cadence
10. Risks, dependencies, evidence gaps, and next decisions
