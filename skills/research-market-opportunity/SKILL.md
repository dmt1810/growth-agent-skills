# Research Market Opportunity

## Objective

Find opportunities that are useful for decisions without overstating the evidence.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision, market boundary, geography, segment, time horizon, and research questions.
2. Inventory available evidence. Separate user-provided data, primary research, authoritative sources, third-party research, and market signals.
3. Research customer problems, jobs, alternatives, buying conditions, competitors, demand, and category dynamics.
4. Triangulate important claims. Prefer primary evidence and independent corroboration.
5. Classify every material claim using the evidence protocol below.
6. Develop opportunities from converging evidence, not isolated observations.
7. Rank opportunities by potential impact, strategic fit, evidence strength, urgency, and validation cost.
8. Specify research required to close every decision-critical evidence gap.

Read [references/evidence-protocol.md](references/evidence-protocol.md) before evaluating claims or presenting opportunities.

## Evidence Protocol

- Never invent survey results, interviews, customer quotes, benchmarks, market sizes, or performance data.
- Attach a source or supplied-data reference to every external factual claim.
- Record source date, relevant geography or segment, and limitations when material.
- Label analysis as **verified evidence**, **corroborated signal**, **reasonable inference**, **unvalidated hypothesis**, or **evidence gap**.
- Treat an unsupported opportunity as unvalidated, not disproven.
- If primary evidence is absent, continue useful desk research but do not represent it as customer validation.
- Recommend concrete validation work instead of filling gaps with assumptions.

## Required Output

1. Decision and scope
2. Executive answer
3. Research method and source coverage
4. Market and category findings
5. Customer problems, jobs, and buying context
6. Competitor and alternative analysis
7. Ranked opportunity table
8. Evidence register: findings, inferences, hypotheses, and gaps
9. Validation plan for unresolved questions
10. Sources with links and access or publication dates

For each opportunity include supporting evidence, evidence status, confidence, missing evidence, validation work, and the decision that validation will unlock.

## Validation Plan Requirements

For each evidence gap specify:

- hypothesis or research question
- recommended method
- target participants or data source
- questions, events, or fields to collect
- sample or coverage rationale
- success and decision thresholds
- expected effort and sequence
- decision unlocked

Do not prescribe a universal sample size. Explain why the proposed coverage is sufficient for the decision and research method.
